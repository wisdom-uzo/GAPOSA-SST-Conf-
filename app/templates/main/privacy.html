Privacy Policy

ICONFST’26 Delegate and Author Data Protection Policy

1. Data Collection

The School of Science and Technology, The Gateway (ICT) Polytechnic Saapade collects personal information including your full name, institutional affiliation, email address, phone number, manuscript files, and payment transaction references solely for organizing the ICONFST’26 conference and associated academic proceedings.

2. Usage of Information

Your details are used to process registrations, conduct double-blind peer review of submissions, generate conference badges and acceptance letters, and communicate essential timetable updates.

3. Data Security & Storage

All data is securely stored using Google Cloud Firestore and Google Firebase Storage services with enterprise-grade encryption. We do not sell or lease your personal data to third parties.
