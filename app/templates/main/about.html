About The Conference

Bridging Academia and Industrial Frontiers

The School of Science and Technology at The Gateway (ICT) Polytechnic Saapade presents ICONFST’26.

Conference Overview

Sustainable Research from Gown to Town

The International Conference on Science and Technology (ICONFST’26) is a premier interdisciplinary forum that convenes global researchers, industrialists, innovators, and policy leaders.

Our core mandate is to translate academic discoveries into viable, sustainable, and scalable industrial solutions. In an era where technological innovation is essential for national and global sustainability, ICONFST’26 provides a robust bridge between scholarly inquiry and industry adoption.

Host Institution

The Gateway (ICT) Polytechnic Saapade, Ogun State

The Gateway (ICT) Polytechnic Saapade (GAPOSA) is a leading ICT-driven technological institution established by the Ogun State Government. Renowned for its cutting-edge computing infrastructure, technical excellence, and dedication to practical skill acquisition, GAPOSA continues to produce innovators, entrepreneurs, and scholars who excel globally.

The School of Science and Technology is the scientific heartbeat of the institution, housing departments across Computer Science, Science Laboratory Technology, Statistics, Food Technology, and Applied Sciences.
