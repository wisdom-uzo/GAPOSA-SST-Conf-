Terms & Conditions

Participation, Registration & Submission Rules

1. Submission & Copyright

By submitting a manuscript to ICONFST’26, the authors certify that the work is original and does not infringe upon any third-party intellectual property rights. Authors retain copyright while granting the conference committee permission to publish the work in the official conference proceedings.

2. Registration & Cancellation

All participants and presenting authors must complete registration and provide proof of payment into the designated Zenith Bank account. Registration fees are non-refundable once the technical program has been published.

3. Code of Conduct

Delegates are expected to maintain the highest standards of academic integrity, mutual respect, and professional conduct during both physical sessions at Prince Dapo Abiodun CON Leadership Hall and virtual broadcast tracks.
