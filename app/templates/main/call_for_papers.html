Author Instructions

Call for Papers & Author Guidelines

Submit your research for double-blind peer review and proceedings publication at ICONFST’26.

Crucial Deadline Abstract Submission Cutoff

31ST JULY, 2026

Authors must submit their abstracts and preliminary manuscripts on or before this date.

Submit Paper Now

i. Length of Paper

Full manuscripts must not exceed 12 pages in total, inclusive of all tables, diagrams, references, and appendices.

ii. Line Spacing

The body of the paper must be formatted using 1.5 line spacing, with single line spacing for the abstract block.

iii. Typography

The entire document must be set in 12pt Times New Roman typeface. Headings may be bolded with appropriate hierarchy.

iv. Referencing Style

All citations and reference lists must strictly adhere to the APA 7th Edition referencing standard.

v. Abstract & Keywords

The abstract must be not more than 200 words, written with single line spacing, accompanied by 3 to 5 keywords.

vi. Originality & Review

Papers must be original, non-plagiarized works. All submissions will be evaluated through double-blind peer review by our international editorial board.
